About Alibaba Composite Textile Mills Ltd

Sustainably Transforming Global Textile Manufacturing

Alibaba Composite Textile Mills Ltd is one of Bangladesh's premier textile innovators, bridging advanced weaving technology with precision chemical dyeing and sustainable manufacturing. We partner with global apparel retailers, garment manufacturers, uniform contractors, and export trading houses.

"We envision a global textile industry that protects and restores the environment and enhances lives." Operating from our advanced composite facility in Tarabo, Rupganj, Narayanganj and our corporate commercial headquarters in Uttara, Dhaka, we uphold uncompromising standards in fiber integrity, weave durability, and color fastness.

Continuous Dyeing & Finishing Unit

Located at 2 No Pathan Para, Pagar, Tongi, Gazipur, our advanced dyeing unit delivers continuous pre-treatment, thermosol dyeing, soft-flow reactive processing, and specialized garment finish treatments with strict shade-matching protocols.

Our Modern Journey

Evolution of a Next-Gen Textile Powerhouse

From our founding in 2020 to a full-scale composite woven textile enterprise operating 200 looms and continuous dyeing lines trusted worldwide.

2020

Enterprise Foundation & Vision

Alibaba Composite Textile Mills Ltd was established in Bangladesh, founded to deliver export-grade woven fabrics with modern, energy-efficient manufacturing standards.

2021

Weaving Shed & 150 Rapier Looms Setup

Commissioned the primary weaving plant at 1 No Dighi Barabo, Tarabo Bishwaroad, Rupganj with 150 high-precision rapier looms, producing heavy cotton twill, canvas, and poplin.

2022

Air-Jet Technology & Scale Expansion (200 Looms)

Installed 50 ultra-high-speed air-jet looms, expanding the total fleet to 200 looms and boosting monthly weaving capacity to 1,500,000 yards (50,000 yards/day).

2023

Continuous Dyeing & Finishing Mill (Tongi, Gazipur)

Commissioned our continuous dyeing and finishing complex in Tongi, Gazipur with continuous pad-steam dyeing ranges and stenter machinery delivering 120,000 yards per day.

2024

DataColor Lab & Technical Fabric Finishes

Inaugurated an in-house quality control testing laboratory with DataColor spectrophotometers, adding technical finishes including peach skin, water repellency, and flame retardancy.

2025 – Present

Global Export Leadership & Sustainable Future

Achieved 18,000,000 yards annual capacity, establishing strategic export supplier partnerships with leading multinational buying houses across Europe, America, and Asia.
